\section*{Data Description}

The data form a reproducible daily panel for studying the reorientation of
Russia's exchange-traded FX market toward CNY/RUB, derivatives pricing relative
to funding costs, market-pressure proxies, and configurable sanctions-related
regimes. ``USD/EUR collapse'' means declining exchange activity and liquidity,
not necessarily depreciation of the currencies.

\subsection*{Page 2: Funding, pricing, options, and validation}

\begin{table}[ht]
\small
\caption{Maturity matching and futures pricing}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.2cm} X}
\toprule
Item & Definition \\
\midrule
Quotation/time & RUB per one foreign-currency unit; $T=(\text{expiry}-\text{trade date})/365$. \\
Rate match & Backward as-of closest tenor. Market-rate staleness is at most 14 calendar days; monthly LPR allows 45. Tenor, distance, date, staleness, method, and source family are stored. \\
Theoretical price & $F^{*}_{t,T}=S_t\exp[(r^{RUB}_{t,T}-r^{foreign}_{t,T})T]$, using annual rates in decimals. Pricing outputs are populated for CNY/RUB; USD/EUR histories support activity comparisons. \\
Deviations & Level, percentage, log and annualized basis; implied, observed, and excess funding differentials; convergence change. \\
Boundary & A positive basis is not executable-arbitrage proof without costs, margin, funding access, controls, sanctions, settlement restrictions, and convertibility. \\

\begin{table}[ht]
\small
\caption{Option model and volatility surface}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.2cm} X}
\toprule
Item & Implementation \\
\midrule
Scope & Vanilla calls/puts whose exact MOEX \texttt{UNDERLYINGASSET} is a CNY/RUB futures SECID. 5,346 spot-underlying option rows are preserved in raw/audit data but excluded. \\
Price hierarchy & Bid--ask midpoint, settlement, VWAP, close. Historical bid/ask are unavailable, so settlement normally has priority. \\
Model & Black--76 with futures-style discount factor one. MOEX American early exercise is not modelled and is a documented approximation. \\
Delta & Unadjusted futures delta: $N(d_1)$ for calls and $N(d_1)-1$ for puts; not OTC spot, forward, or premium-adjusted delta. \\
Surface & ATM minimizes $|\ln(K/F)|$. Nearest actual 25-delta and 10-delta call/put observations support risk reversal and butterfly; counts disclose support and no interpolation is presented as a quote. \\
Rejections & Invalid maturity/price, missing dated underlying, no-arbitrage-bound violation, or no stable IV in the configured 0.01\%--500\% range. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
