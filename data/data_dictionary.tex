\section*{Processed CNY/RUB Field Dictionary}

The definitions below apply to both \field{contract_daily.csv} and
\field{nearby_daily.csv}. The former contains one contract-date row and
calculates futures returns within \field{secid}. The latter selects the
non-expired contract with the shortest remaining maturity, adds
\field{contract_changed}, and leaves its futures return missing on selection
changes. Header order does not change field meaning.

\subsection*{Identifiers and contract dates}
\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.25\textwidth}p{0.17\textwidth}p{0.52\textwidth}@{}}
\tablehead
\field{trade_date} & Date & Trading-session date for the row's futures, spot, and matched funding data. \\
\field{boardid} & Text & MOEX board identifier; \field{RFUD} is the FORTS futures board used here. \\
\field{secid} & Text & Exact MOEX futures-contract identifier and key for within-contract calculations. \\
\field{shortname} & Text & MOEX short name reported in daily history, for example \field{CNY-9.22}. \\
\field{assetcode} & Text & Underlying-asset code; \field{CNY} identifies Chinese-yuan futures. \\
\field{shortname_meta} & Text & Short name obtained separately from contract-reference metadata and retained for validation. \\
\field{expiry_date} & Date & Delivery/expiration date from MOEX \field{LSTDELDATE}. \\
\field{first_trade_date} & Date & First date on which MOEX lists the contract as tradable. \\
\field{last_trade_date} & Date & Scheduled last trading date from MOEX \field{LSTTRADE}, kept separately from expiry. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

Rates are matched backward as-of with no future information and at most 14 calendar days of staleness.

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.25\textwidth}p{0.17\textwidth}p{0.52\textwidth}@{}}
\tablehead

\field{rub_tenor_distance_days} & Calendar days & Absolute difference between maturity days and selected RUB nominal tenor. \\
\field{rub_rate_observation_date} & Date & Observation/publication date of the RUB rate used in the row. \\

\subsection*{Derived pricing, basis, and returns}
\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.25\textwidth}p{0.17\textwidth}p{0.52\textwidth}@{}}
\tablehead
\field{theoretical_futures_price} & RUB/CNY & $S_t\exp[((r^{RUB}-r^{CNY})/100)T]$ under continuous compounding and Actual/365. Missing if a required valid input is unavailable. \\
\field{basis_rub_per_cny} & RUB/CNY & Observed futures price minus theoretical price; positive means observed exceeds the benchmark. \\
\field{basis_pct} & Percent & 100 times the level basis divided by theoretical price. \\
\field{log_basis} & Decimal & $\ln(F_{observed}/F_{theoretical})$, defined only for positive prices. \\
\field{futures_log_return} & Decimal & Within-\field{secid} log return in the contract panel; nearby-price log change in the nearby panel, missing on contract changes. \\
\field{contract_changed} & Boolean; nearby only & True on the first nearby row and whenever selected \field{secid} differs from the preceding date. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection*{Extended three-pair, funding, pressure, and option files}

The repository also contains \field{fx_spot_panel.csv},
\field{futures_pricing_panel.csv}, \field{fx_nearby_daily.csv},
\field{funding_curve_daily.csv}, \field{market_pressure_daily.csv},
\field{currency_market_share_daily.csv}, \field{options_panel.csv},
\field{volatility_surface_daily.csv}, and
\field{sanctions_regime_daily.csv}. The Markdown dictionary explains these
field families and \field{docs/variable_catalog.csv} provides, for every raw,
processed, and analysis column, its source, original field, unit, frequency,
transformation, missing-value treatment, economic interpretation, and main
limitation.

Options are restricted to exact CNY/RUB futures underlyings. Black--76 uses a
futures-style discount factor of one and unadjusted futures delta. Historical
bid and ask are not available, so the price hierarchy normally begins with
settlement. Invalid maturity, price, underlying, no-arbitrage bound, or implied
volatility solutions are retained with a rejection reason. Surface ATM,
25-delta, and 10-delta values are nearest actual observations, not interpolated
quotes.

\subsection*{Interpretation notes}
Missing observations are not automatically zeros. Volume, turnover, open
interest count, and open-interest value are distinct. Closing, settlement, and
weighted-average prices are also distinct; \field{futures_price} records the
selection rule. Finally, a cost-of-carry basis is a pricing deviation rather
than proof of executable arbitrage after funding access, capital controls,
margin, spreads, settlement, and sanctions constraints.
