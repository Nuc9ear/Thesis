\section*{Data Description}

This study uses a reproducible, public-source dataset to examine the pricing, trading activity, funding conditions, and derivative-market structure of the Chinese yuan against the Russian ruble. It combines official observations from the Moscow Exchange (MOEX), the Bank of Russia, the China Foreign Exchange Trade System (CFETS/ChinaMoney), and the People's Bank of China (PBOC). The central window is 1 July 2022--28 July 2026, with longer histories used to establish pre-event patterns. The main market-regime break is 13 June 2024, the first trading day after the designation of MOEX, the National Clearing Centre, and the National Settlement Depository.

The core CNY/RUB panel is built from MOEX Information and Statistical Server records. It contains 6,078 contract-day observations for 22 futures contracts between 1 July 2022 and 27 July 2026, together with 1,036 daily spot observations and 6,494 long-format funding observations. Prices are in rubles per yuan. Futures price is selected from settlement, volume-weighted average price, and close, in that order; spot uses the volume-weighted price and then the close. The data retain contract identifiers, expiration and time to maturity, OHLC and settlement prices, volume, ruble turnover, open interest, and number of trades. A continuous nearby series selects the non-expired contract with the shortest remaining maturity. It spans 1,036 trading days, 17 nearby contracts, and 16 rolls; returns are missing on roll dates to avoid treating contract changes as daily price movements. A focal file contains 1,511 observations for CRM6, CRU6, CRZ6, CRH7, CRM7, and CRU7.

To place CNY/RUB in the broader foreign-exchange market, a canonical spot panel covers CNY/RUB, USD/RUB, USD/CNY, EUR/RUB, and EUR/CNY from January 2013 to 28 July 2026. It has 20,161 pair-day observations: 4,111, 4,037, 4,013, 4,012, and 3,988 for the respective pairs. A positive-price, positive-trade direct MOEX observation is preferred; otherwise, a cross-implied MOEX value and then a Bank of Russia official or cross rate are used. Direct exchange rows retain OHLC, volume-weighted price, and trade count. USD/CNY and EUR/CNY cross highs and lows are labelled as bounds because component extrema need not be simultaneous. Bank of Russia values are reference rates, not exchange closes, and returns are not calculated across source changes.

The spot database also contains 3,424 daily activity rows with MOEX trade counts and CNY/RUB's share of activity among ruble pairs. For comparison, a separate MOEX archive covers USD/RUB (\texttt{Si}) and EUR/RUB (\texttt{Eu}) futures from 2009 through 2021. It contains 134 contracts, 32,109 contract-day observations (20,067 USD/RUB and 12,042 EUR/RUB), and 6,472 market-day nearby observations. Original ruble-per-1,000-unit prices are preserved and normalized per dollar or euro. This archive provides a pre-2022 benchmark without joining differently specified contracts to the CNY/RUB sample.

Funding conditions are organized as a 1,489-row as-of daily curve. It combines MOEX RUSFAR ruble and yuan tenors; the Bank of Russia key rate, RUONIA, and term RUONIA averages; SHIBOR from overnight to one year; CFETS repo fixings including FDR007; monthly one- and five-year loan prime rates; and seven PBOC changes in the seven-day reverse-repo policy rate. The source series include 1,037 key-rate days, 1,004 RUONIA observations, 1,015 SHIBOR days, 1,015 repo-fixing days, and 51 monthly LPR observations. Values are carried forward for at most 14 calendar days (45 for LPR), and RUONIA is merged by publication date to prevent look-ahead. Each futures maturity is matched to the closest funding tenor; observation dates and tenor distances remain available for audit.

The processed contract panels compare observed futures prices with a continuous-compounding cost-of-carry benchmark,
\[
F^{*}_{t,T}=S_t\exp\!\left[(r^{\mathrm{RUB}}_{t,T}-r^{\mathrm{CNY}}_{t,T})T\right],
\]
where $S_t$ is CNY/RUB spot, the two rates proxy ruble and yuan funding costs, and $T$ is Actual/365 time to expiration. Observed-minus-theoretical basis is reported in rubles per yuan, percentages, and logs. A robustness specification uses the nearest SHIBOR tenor, while a within-contract 60-observation z-score identifies unusual deviations. The basis is not automatically an arbitrage profit because capital controls, yuan funding access, margin, sanctions, settlement risk, spreads, and transaction costs are not fully observed.

The derivatives extension contains weekly MOEX option cross-sections on CNY/RUB futures: 30,698 metadata rows and 330,174 raw contract-snapshot observations over 214 dates from 1 July 2022 to 27 July 2026. Requiring positive volume or open interest and valid model outputs yields 13,570 strike-expiry surface points and 2,009 summary observations. Variables include type, strike, expiry, underlying, settlement, volume, turnover, open interest, log-moneyness, Black--76 implied volatility, and futures delta. Summaries provide ATM volatility, 25-delta risk reversals and butterflies, put/call ratios, and ATM implied variance less trailing realized spot variance. Since the listed contracts are American-style, futures-style options, Black--76 is an approximation; its futures delta is not an OTC spot or premium-adjusted delta.

Event indicators, regime labels, and distance from 13 June 2024 are included throughout. In the 90 calendar days before suspension, CNY/RUB averaged 54,339 MOEX trades per observed day versus 29,355 combined USD/RUB and EUR/RUB trades. During suspension, CNY/RUB averaged 62,520 while recorded exchange trading in the other two pairs fell to zero. From 16 February 2026, 112 positive-price USD/RUB rows reappear, classified as ruble-settled and non-deliverable rather than a continuation of the former market.

Interpretation is subject to several limits. Free MOEX spot history reports price and trade count, but not pair-level volume, turnover, bid, ask, or spreads; \texttt{NUMTRADES} is only an activity measure. Weekly option rows are snapshot-day values, not weekly flows. Bank of Russia rates are references rather than executable prices, FDR007 is a morning fixing rather than full-day DR007, and the six focal futures trade only after the sanctions break. Raw, processed, and manifest files preserve sources, dates, units, and construction rules. Validation found no duplicate processed keys or non-positive canonical prices; 162 contract-day rows lack a sufficiently recent yuan funding observation and thus a theoretical price.
