\section*{Data Description}

The collected data form a reproducible public-source panel for analysing CNY/RUB pricing, market activity, funding conditions, derivatives, and the structural break of 13 June 2024.

\textbf{Table 1. Research design and coverage}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.20\textwidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Aspect} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
Research purpose & CNY/RUB spot and futures pricing, cost of carry, market activity, option-implied risk, and changes surrounding the June 2024 sanctions event. \\
Official sources & Moscow Exchange (MOEX); Bank of Russia; China Foreign Exchange Trade System (CFETS/ChinaMoney); People's Bank of China (PBOC). \\
Main window & 1 July 2022--28 July 2026; MOEX futures and spot observations end on 27 July where 28 July was not yet a trading-data date. \\
Extended histories & Five-pair spot panel from January 2013; USD/RUB and EUR/RUB futures from 2009 through 2021. \\
Main event date & 13 June 2024, the first trading day after the designation of MOEX, the National Clearing Centre, and the National Settlement Depository. \\
Conventions & CNY/RUB is RUB per CNY; maturity uses calendar days and Actual/365 years. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\textbf{Table 2. Market datasets collected}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.17\textwidth}p{0.22\textwidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Component} & \textbf{Coverage and size} & \textbf{Contents and research use} \\
\midrule
CNY/RUB futures & 22 contracts; 6,078 contract-day rows; 1 July 2022--27 July 2026 & Identifier, expiry, maturity, OHLC, settlement, volume, RUB turnover, open interest, and trades. Price uses settlement, then VWAP, then close. \\
CNY/RUB spot & 1,036 daily observations & MOEX VWAP, then close; used for returns, volatility, cost of carry, and basis. \\
Nearby futures & 1,036 days; 17 contracts; 16 rolls & Shortest observed non-expired maturity. Returns are missing on roll dates. \\
Six focal maturities & 1,511 rows: CRM6, CRU6, CRZ6, CRH7, CRM7, and CRU7 & Post-event prices, activity, funding matches, theoretical values, basis, and event fields. \\
Canonical spot FX & 20,161 pair-days through 28 July 2026 & CNY/RUB: 4,111; USD/RUB: 4,037; USD/CNY: 4,013; EUR/RUB: 4,012; EUR/CNY: 3,988. \\
Spot activity & 3,424 daily rows & MOEX trade counts and CNY/RUB share of activity among RUB pairs. \\
Historical futures & 134 contracts; 32,109 contract-days; 6,472 nearby market-days; 2009--2021 & USD/RUB: 20,067 rows; EUR/RUB: 12,042. RUB-per-1,000 prices retained and normalized per unit. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\textbf{Table 5. Constructed analytical variables}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.23\textwidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Variable} & \textbf{Construction and interpretation} \\
\midrule
Theoretical futures & $F^{*}_{t,T}=S_t\exp[(r^{\mathrm{RUB}}_{t,T}-r^{\mathrm{CNY}}_{t,T})T]$; $T$ is Actual/365 maturity. \\
Futures basis & Observed minus theoretical price in RUB per CNY, percentage, and log form. \\
SHIBOR basis & Replaces the MOEX CNY rate with the closest SHIBOR tenor. \\
Basis z-score & Within-contract 60-observation standardization. \\
Returns/volatility & Contract and spot log returns; annualized rolling 20-observation spot volatility. \\
Activity measures & Trade counts, CNY share, pre-event ratios, and activity-pressure z-scores. \\
Event variables & Pre/post indicator, regime, and distance from 13 June 2024. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\textbf{Table 7. Event evidence, limitations, and validation}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.22\textwidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Item} & \textbf{Result or interpretation boundary} \\
\midrule
Pre-suspension & CNY/RUB averaged 54,339 trades per observed day; USD/RUB plus EUR/RUB averaged 29,355. \\
Suspension & CNY/RUB averaged 62,520; recorded USD/RUB and EUR/RUB exchange trading fell to zero. \\
2026 USD/RUB & 112 positive-price rows from 16 February; classified as RUB-settled and non-deliverable. \\
Spot limitation & No pair-level historical volume, turnover, bid, ask, or spread. \texttt{NUMTRADES} is not volume. \\
Option limitation & Weekly snapshots are not weekly flows. Black--76 approximates American-style, futures-style options. \\
Pricing limitation & Basis is not proof of arbitrage; access constraints and trading costs are unobserved. \\
Sample limitation & Six focal futures trade only after June 2024. \\
Validation & No duplicate processed keys or non-positive prices; 162 rows lack recent CNY funding and theoretical price. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
